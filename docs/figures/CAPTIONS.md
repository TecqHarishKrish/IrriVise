# IrriVise / CARAT: figures and captions

Every figure is generated by `scripts/make_figures.py` (`python scripts/make_figures.py --out docs/figures`).
Figures 1–3 read only the saved results in `docs/results/`; Figures 4–5 re-run the simulator on held-out
test season 2020, seed 1 (the first test season and seed, not chosen for effect). PNG at 300 dpi for Word,
PDF (vector) for LaTeX. All outcomes come from a simulated farm and synthetic weather, not field data.

**Fig. 1. Main benchmark on held-out test seasons.** (a) Yield proxy, (b) seasonal irrigation and (c) soil readings
per season for eleven methods: baselines B0–B5, ablations A5–A6, the proposed Full CARAT (P), the CARAT planner without
PPO (P-noPPO) and a daily-reading reference (R). Dots are means and bars 95 % bootstrap confidence intervals over 20
matched cells (seasons 2020–2023 × seeds 1–5), budget 10 readings, sensor noise 0.02 m³/m³. Proposed methods in blue.
Source: `docs/results/main_benchmark.json`.

**Fig. 2. Robustness of CARAT's reading policy to budget and sensor noise.** Paired difference in total reward (excluding
reading cost) between Full CARAT and PPO with fixed-interval readings (B3) under the same PPO policy, for budgets of 5–40
readings and sensor noise σ = 0.01–0.04 m³/m³. Points above zero favour CARAT; bars are 95 % confidence intervals over
4 test seasons × 3 seeds per cell. Source: `docs/results/robustness_breakdown.md`.

**Fig. 3. Effect of sensor-bias handling in the digital twin under sensor faults.** Mean yield proxy of (a) the CARAT
planner and (b) the soil-moisture threshold rule with no bias handling, an innovation (CUSUM) monitor, and the
augmented sensor-offset state, for a healthy sensor, 25 % dropout, ±0.03 m³/m³ bias and a stuck sensor. Dashed line:
fixed schedule without readings (B0). 20 matched cells per bar. Source: `docs/results/sensor_bias_nonppo_test.json`.

**Fig. 4. Digital-twin estimate under a wet-biased sensor in one test season.** Twin estimate (±1 sd) against the hidden
true root-zone water for the CARAT planner when every reading is 0.03 m³/m³ too wet. (a) Without bias handling the twin is
pulled too wet in mid-season, the planner under-irrigates and the crop is stressed. (b) The augmented offset state
attributes the persistent disagreement to the sensor, avoiding the wet error and the stress, at the cost of a slightly dry
twin. Season 2020, seed 1.

**Fig. 5. One season of CARAT decisions with a healthy sensor.** (a) Digital twin (±1 sd) against the hidden truth, with
the six soil readings CARAT chose out of a budget of ten. (b) Estimated regret and net value of information (VOI); a
reading is taken only when net VOI is positive. (c) Daily rain and the irrigation applied. Season 2020, seed 1.
